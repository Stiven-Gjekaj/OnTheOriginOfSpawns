The husk and the drowned evolve. Each one has 30 genes, 5 families, 20 minor families, and 10 hybrids of its own. They share no genes with the zombie.

This is an alpha. Expect balance changes, and use a test world. The [wiki](https://stiven-gjekaj.github.io/OnTheOriginOfSpawns/) shows each gene and family.

> **Content warning: body horror.** Some gene mixes show bare bone, open wounds, stitched gashes, blood, rot, fungus, and veins on the skin.

## The husk
- **Scorchers** are strong in the sun and weak in the dark. Sunbaked heals in sunlight. Heatshimmer makes projectiles miss and go through it. Solar Flare blinds players when it dies in daylight.
- **Dunecrawlers** live in the sand. Sandswimmer rises out of the sand next to you. Scuttler climbs walls, but not glass. Duststorm gives Darkness to players near it.
- **Mummies** are hard to kill. Undying survives one lethal hit with a burst of sand. Wrapped resists projectiles and melee. Cursetouch gives Weakness. Embalmed ignores Poison, Wither, and Hunger. Tomb Guard cannot be knocked back. Grave Dust leaves a cloud of Slowness when it dies.
- **Caravans** travel in groups. Linked takes less damage near other husks. Signal Fire sends up smoke and calls the husks near it. Nomad leads a line of husks. Sandling spawns as a baby husk.
- **Parchers** take your water and your food. Thirstbite and Dehydrator drain your hunger. Mirage Water shows a false pool of water and is hard to see. Desiccate gives Hunger to players near it. Salt Crust stops a husk from turning into a zombie in water.

## The drowned
- **Riptides** are fast in water. Surge dashes at you through the water. Breacher jumps out of the water at you on the shore, on a dock, or in a boat. Undertow pulls you down. Capsizer breaks your boat.
- **Harpooners** throw tridents. Volley throws two times as often. Barbed tridents give Slowness. Spearfisher throws farther at players in water or in boats. Scavenger picks up the tridents that you throw. Stormcaller calls lightning with its trident in a storm.
- **Abyssals** come from the dark and the deep. Lure hangs a light that goes out when you come near, and its first hit does double damage. Pressure Hide is tough in the deep. Blind Hunter finds invisible players, but not a player who sneaks. Ink blinds you when you hit it.
- **Barnacled** grow shells. Coral Growth hurts you when you hit it. Shellback takes less damage from behind. Anchor sinks fast. Pearlheart heals to full one time. Kelpwrapped is almost invisible in kelp.
- **Sirens** sing. The family pulls players in water toward it. Siren Song slows swimmers. Drag Under holds you and sinks with you. Chorus calls the drowned near it. Fry spawns as a baby drowned.

## Equipment
- Mobs with genes no longer spawn with random weapons or random armor, and they do not pick up items.
- Natural weapons stay: the trident and the fishing rod of the drowned, and the iron spear of a husk that rides a camel husk.
- Genes can give items: Tridentborn and the Harpooners give tridents, and Packmule picks up items.

## Babies and conversion
- Baby husks come only from Sandling, and baby drowned come only from Fry.
- A mob that converts to a different species, for example a zombie that drowns, gets new genes from the pool of the new species. It keeps none of its old genes.

## Living evolution
- A mob that lives for one Minecraft day or more can swap its weakest gene for a gene that its pool now favors. This happens only where no player is near and no player can see it.
- A mob with a name tag never changes its genes.

## Knowledge
- You see the family of a mob after you watch it for 8 seconds in 16 blocks.
- You learn the genes of a mob when you kill it, or when it kills you.
- The mob info panel and the Beagle Notebook show only what you know.

## Fixes
- Burrowing zombies now rise to the surface and stop for 2 seconds, as the design says.
- Stalkers have quieter steps at tier 1, and no death sound at tier 3. Their first hit bonus counts only on players.
- Shade is see-through at night in any light.
- A Lunger leap that reaches you now hits.
- A frenzied zombie no longer attacks husks or drowned.

## Data packs
- New effect types for water, tridents, boats, senses, and defense, and new states such as `on_land`, `deep`, `storm`, and `sunlit`.
- Many effects have new options, for example `players_only`, `cooldown_seconds`, and `night_only`.
- The [data pack guide](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/OnTheOriginOfSpawns/blob/main/docs/data-packs.md) describes each field.

## Upgrade
- Zombies from 0.2.0-alpha keep their genes. Husks and drowned from 0.2.0-alpha get genes when they next load.
- The families that you found in the Beagle Notebook stay found.

## Requirements
- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or later
- Fabric API
