A fix for the looks of two drowned genes.

## Fixes
- **Torrent:** the fins now sit on the lower legs and do not float. The side fins face outward on both legs.
- **Undertow:** the kelp is now drawn on the head and the back, in place of a model.

## Requirements
- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or later
- Fabric API
